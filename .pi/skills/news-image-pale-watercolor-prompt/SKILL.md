# Pale watercolor news image

Compile only. Do not generate images, invoke Tools, read files, access the network or write artifacts. Return only the JSON requested by the Agent.

Treat the title and summary as the complete factual source. Preserve the event, core tension and minimum reality anchor; do not invent facts. Freeze one physical Scene Kernel with one object acting on, passing, supporting, finding or changing one other object. Do not create an icon list.

Create a wordless East Asian pale-watercolor picture-book image with one enormous quiet stone or natural presence, one to three tiny actors, an asymmetric route through a threshold or gap, warm old paper, pale-cyan air, gray-green mass, muted yellow-green ground and a small dark-earth foreground accent. Use thin uneven warm-brown ink and transparent washes. Keep at least one third breathing. Do not use identifiable landmarks or literal enlargement of the news object.

Compile a 300–8,000 character English prompt with these headings in order: `Use case: stylized-concept`, `Asset type: horizontal editorial main visual, composed safely for a 4:3 crop`, `NEWS RELATION:`, `ONE SCENE KERNEL:`, `COMPOSITION:`, `PHYSICAL MAKING RECIPE:`, `TEXT:`, `NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS:`.

The recipe must begin on warm old paper with uneven warm-brown ink; add transparent pale-cyan sky and diluted moss or gray-green land washes; crosshatch sparingly; keep distant contours open, actors tiny and at least one third breathing; stop before cinematic lighting, polished concept art or full coverage.

The `TEXT` section must contain exactly: `No text, letters, numbers, logos or pseudo-text anywhere in the image.` Reject text, watermarks, HUDs, polished concept art, cinematic lighting, heroic close-ups, landmarks, pagodas, temples, ceremonial gates, fantasy costumes and a second metaphor.

Return exactly `newsIdentity`, `coreTension`, `realityAnchor`, `factExclusions`, `sceneKernel`, and `prompt` as JSON.
